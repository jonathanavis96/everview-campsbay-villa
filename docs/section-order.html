What I would recommend, and why

The old order put the full photo index (“The plates”) third, which buried everything after it — a guest scrolling past sixty photographs rarely reaches the reviews, the rate or the enquiry form. It also opened the power-and-water section before the guest had seen the house.

The recommended order follows the decision a guest is actually making: want it → believe it → work out the practicalities → see the price → ask. Desire first (the house, then the bedrooms), then where it is, then the reassurances an overseas guest needs, then proof, then price, then the operational detail, then the enquiry. The plate index becomes an appendix at the end, where a long index belongs.
